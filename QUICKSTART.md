# FIFA 27 - Quick Start Guide

Welcome to FIFA 27! This guide will help you get started in just a few minutes.

## 🚀 Getting Started

1. **Open the game**: Open `index.html` in your web browser
2. **You're ready to play!** No installation needed.

## 📺 First Time Tips

### Your First Game Mode

When you start, you'll see 4 main options:

#### **🏆 Ultimate Team** (Recommended for beginners)
- Start with 100,000 coins and 5,000 FUT Points
- Your squad comes pre-loaded with 11 players
- Buy more players from the market to upgrade
- Compete in online divisions

**First Steps:**
1. Click "Ultimate Team"
2. Go to "Squad" tab to see your current team
3. Go to "Packs" tab to open packs and get new players
4. Go to "Division" tab and click "Play Match" to start playing
5. Use Shoot, Pass, and Defend buttons to play
6. Complete the match to earn coins and rewards

#### **🌍 World Cup**
- Tournament-based gameplay
- Compete with all world nations
- Progress through group stage and knockout rounds
- Win the World Cup!

#### **⭐ Champions League**
- Compete with Europe's top clubs
- Group stage + Knockout rounds
- Battle for the title

#### **📊 Career Mode**
- Manage one of 10 top European clubs
- Compete in domestic leagues
- Build a squad dynasty
- Work with transfer budget

## 💰 Economy Basics

### Starting Resources
- **Coins**: 100,000 (used for player purchases)
- **FUT Points**: 5,000 (premium currency)

### How to Earn Coins
- **Win a match**: 1,500+ coins
- **Draw a match**: 1,000 coins
- **Lose a match**: 500 coins
- **Open packs**: Sell unwanted players

### Pack Prices
| Pack | Cost |
|------|------|
| Bronze | 400 coins |
| Silver | 1,500 coins |
| Gold | 5,000 coins |
| Elite | 15,000 coins |
| Premium | 50,000 coins |

## 🎮 Playing Your First Match

1. **Choose Mode** → Select "Ultimate Team"
2. **Go to Divisions** → Click "Play Match"
3. **You're in!** The match screen appears
4. **Match Actions:**
   - **⚽ Shoot** - Try to score (20-40% success rate)
   - **→ Pass** - Build possession and move forward
   - **🛡️ Defend** - Block opponent attacks
   - **⏭️ Skip** - Speed through the rest of the match

5. **Win/Lose/Draw** - Match ends, you get rewards

## 📊 Understanding the Squad

### Squad Screen
- Shows your 11 starting players in your formation
- Displays each player's **Rating** (overall ability)
- Shows **Chemistry** dots (team cohesion)

### Building Chemistry
- Players from same **nation**: +2 chemistry each
- Players from same **club**: +1 chemistry each
- Higher chemistry = better team performance

### Improving Your Squad
1. **Go to Market tab**
2. **Search** for specific players
3. **Buy** players you need
4. **Add them** to your squad
5. **Arrange formation** for best chemistry

## 🏆 Division System

### How Divisions Work
- **Start**: Division 10 (beginner friendly)
- **Progress**: Win matches to earn points
- **Win**: +3 points
- **Draw**: +1 point
- **Lose**: 0 points

### Getting Better
- A higher squad rating helps you win
- Better difficulty means better rewards
- Play more matches to improve your skill

## ⚙️ Settings Tips
