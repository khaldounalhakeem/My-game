# Boost League — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 31 October 2026_

Boost League does not collect, store or share any personal information.

- **No accounts, no tracking, no ads.** The app has no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no
  third-party tracking.
- **Your progress stays on your device.** Your level, items, ranks and settings are saved only in the app's
  storage on your device. Deleting the app deletes them. If you use a save code, it's text you copy and keep
  yourself.
- **Online parties.** When you create or join a party, your device connects directly to your friends' devices
  to play. To set up that connection, the app uses a public connection service (PeerJS), which sees a random
  connection ID, the party code and your device's network address for as long as the connection is being set
  up. During a match, only game information (your player name, car, team and positions) is sent to the other
  players in your party. Nothing is stored after the party ends.
- **Game controllers** are used only to control the game.
- **Children.** The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

If you have a question about this policy, contact the developer through the App Store listing.
