How to Play

      

        
W A S D move · Space jump · Shift sprint · C sneak

        
Left click mine / attack · Right click place / use / eat

        
E inventory & crafting · 1-9 or wheel: hotbar · Q drop

        
Creative: double-tap Space or F to fly · V camera · Esc pause

        
Touch: tap a block to place, hold on a block to mine, tap a mob to hit, drag to look.

        
PS4 controller: connect it by Bluetooth and press any button.

      

      
    


    

      
About

      
BlockRealm is a fan-made block building game. Find blaze rods in Nether fortresses and ender pearls from endermen, craft Eyes of Ender, follow them to a stronghold, and open the portal to the End to fight the Ender Dragon.
