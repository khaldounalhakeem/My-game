# FIFA 27 - The Ultimate Football Experience

A comprehensive web-based football management and simulation game featuring Ultimate Team, World Cup, Champions League, and Career Mode.

## 🎮 Game Features

### 1. **Ultimate Team Mode** 🏆
- **Squad Building**: Build your dream team with real players
- **Chemistry System**: Earn chemistry bonuses for players from same nation/club
- **Online Divisions**: Compete in competitive divisions (1-10) against AI opponents
- **Player Market**: Buy and sell players on the transfer market
- **Pack Opening**: Open bronze, silver, gold, elite, and premium packs
- **Seasons**: Progress through multiple seasons with rewards
- **Leaderboards**: Compete with other players for top rankings

**Rewards System:**
- Win: 1,500+ coins + XP
- Draw: 1,000 coins + XP
- Loss: 500+ coins + XP

### 2. **World Cup Mode** 🌍
- **Group Stage**: All nations divided into groups
- **Knockout Tournament**: Round of 16, Quarterfinals, Semifinals, Final
- **Tournament Progression**: Track your path to glory
- **Match Statistics**: Goals, shots, possession tracking
- **Rewards**: Coins and special cards for tournament success

### 3. **Champions League Mode** ⭐
- **Group Stage**: 8 groups with multiple clubs
- **Knockout Round**: Elite 16 teams battle for supremacy
- **Tournament Management**: Build your team for European glory
- **Match History**: Track all your CL matches
- **Championship Rewards**: Special rewards for winning the tournament

### 4. **Career Mode** 📊
- **Club Management**: Manage any of 10 major European clubs
- **League Progression**: Compete in domestic leagues
- **Transfer Market**: Buy and sell players for your team
- **Squad Building**: Develop youth players and build dynasty
- **Fixtures Management**: Play through entire seasons
- **Budget Management**: Work with transfer budget constraints
- **League Standing**: Climb the league table

### 5. **Match Engine** ⚽
- **Interactive Gameplay**: Control match pace with decisions
- **Tactical Actions**: Shoot, pass, defend, skip
- **Difficulty Levels**: Easy, Normal, Hard, Legendary
- **Real-Time Statistics**: Possession, shots, passes, fouls tracking
- **Dynamic AI**: Smart opponent adapts to your strategy
- **Goal Variety**: Multiple goal and miss types
- **Match Events**: Commentary-style event updates

## 🏆 Divisions & Rankings

### League Division System
- **Divisions**: 1-10 (10 is starter)

## 📱 How to Play

1. **Start Game**: Launch the application in your browser
2. **Choose Mode**: Select from 4 game modes
3. **Build Team**: Recruit players from the market
4. **Play Matches**: Compete against AI opponents
5. **Earn Rewards**: Gain coins and progress
6. **Upgrade Team**: Buy better players and improve squad

## 🎮 Match Controls

| Action | Effect |
